- `visa-dark.svg`: derived from the existing `visa.svg`; only `fill="white"` is replaced with `fill="#1A1F71"` (the Visa colour specified in Task 132). The adaptation retains CC BY-SA 4.0.
- `troy-dark.svg`: derived from the existing `troy.svg`; only `fill="white"` is replaced with `fill="#0E1510"`.

Paths, viewBoxes, dimensions and proportions are identical to the corresponding originals. `mastercard.svg` keeps its existing brand colours on both surfaces. `PaymentLogos` selects dark artwork for the cart's milk plate and the original light artwork for the dark footer/product-detail surfaces; no new payment brands are introduced.
